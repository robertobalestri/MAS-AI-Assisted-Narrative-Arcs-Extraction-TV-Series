Meredith Grey starts her internship at Seattle Grace while trying to keep her personal life compartmentalized, but that immediately becomes impossible when her one-night stand turns out to be neurosurgeon Derek Shepherd, one of her superiors. On her first day, she clashes and bonds with fellow interns Cristina Yang, George O'Malley, and Izzie Stevens under the brutal supervision of Miranda Bailey. Meredith helps diagnose Katie Bryce's hidden brain aneurysm and earns a place in Derek's OR, which sparks resentment from Cristina and begins the pattern of Meredith's talent being questioned because of her connection to Derek and because she is Ellis Grey's daughter. At home, Meredith reveals the deeper burden she carries: her mother, legendary surgeon Ellis Grey, has Alzheimer's, and Meredith is caring for her in secret.

As the interns settle into the hospital, Meredith reluctantly lets George and Izzie move into her mother's house, creating a chaotic new home life that slowly becomes a support system. Derek keeps pursuing Meredith, and despite her insistence that sleeping with her boss is a terrible idea, they continue circling each other. Meredith is shaken by a rape victim whose case becomes personal, but she also proves herself by pushing concerns about a newborn until Burke takes over the case and lets her assist in surgery. Izzie quietly breaks rules to help an undocumented Chinese girl, George struggles with death and confidence, and Cristina's ambition becomes obvious as she chases every surgical opportunity. Derek and Meredith's attraction keeps escalating even as she tries to resist him.

The interns begin to define themselves through increasingly difficult cases. During the annual bike race disaster, George helps secure a liver for a dying patient, Izzie becomes emotionally invested in a brain-dead John Doe and his family, and Cristina pushes for organ procurement with her usual cold focus before being forced to confront the human side of it. Meredith treats a reckless biker who later nearly dies after ignoring her advice, and Derek makes it clear his interest in her is real, not just a game. At home, the interns' friendships deepen as they invade Meredith's space, watch Ellis Grey's old surgical tapes, and begin functioning like an improvised family.

Meredith's connection to her mother keeps complicating her life at the hospital. Ellis's former scrub nurse Liz Fallon arrives as a patient, and Meredith is hurt that Liz is remembered by Ellis when Meredith herself often is not. Liz's death reinforces Meredith's fear of losing her mother piece by piece. At the same time, Izzie's old modeling career is exposed around the hospital, humiliating her until she reclaims it and insists she earned her way into medicine. George remains awkward and insecure, while Cristina continues to impress Burke enough that a charged, flirtatious dynamic starts forming between them.

Pressure mounts when Meredith, exhausted and distracted by trying to manage Ellis's legal affairs, thinks she may have damaged a patient's heart during surgery. At the same time, George and Cristina uncover that a towel was left inside another patient years earlier and that Burke was involved in that operation. Meredith confesses her possible mistake, exposing herself to discipline, but the truth is that the patient's heart was dangerously weakened by extreme weight loss. When Meredith faces the Chief and hospital lawyers, Burke protects her by admitting his own past error in the retained towel case, arguing that fear should not stop doctors from speaking up. Meredith avoids being fired but is put under observation. That same period also pushes several relationships forward: Izzie's boyfriend Hank cannot accept that surgery comes before him and leaves, Cristina and Burke finally sleep together, and Meredith and Derek continue their affair despite Bailey's obvious disapproval.

Bailey's suspicion that Derek is favoring Meredith becomes a major problem. Meredith tries to pull away because she knows every opportunity she gets will be seen through that lens, but Derek keeps choosing her when she earns it, which only makes things worse. During a day dominated by a massive tumor case and a risky brain procedure, Meredith proves herself repeatedly, while George finally realizes he has waited too long to tell Meredith how he feels. By the time he works up the nerve, she is already fully involved with Derek.

The interns' personal entanglements grow messier. George and Izzie discover Derek sneaking out of Meredith's house, confirming the affair. Izzie initially resents Meredith for sleeping with an attending and seeming to benefit from it, but she softens when she realizes Meredith is genuinely in love with Derek. George reacts with jealousy. Meanwhile, Cristina starts feeling ill and eventually takes two pregnancy tests, discovering she is pregnant. She keeps it secret while continuing her intense, emotionally detached relationship with Burke.

Meredith and Derek's relationship becomes more serious, but Meredith is frustrated that she knows almost nothing about his real life because he is always at her house and never brings her into his world. Eventually, after she pushes him to prove this is a real relationship, Derek takes her to his trailer and starts opening up. In parallel, Cristina quietly arranges an abortion and refuses to discuss alternatives, while still avoiding telling Burke about the pregnancy. Burke and Cristina's bond deepens anyway, built on surgical respect, secrecy, and sex.

Other long-running tensions continue to shape the group. George's confidence rises and falls: he improves clinically, gets praise for speaking up when an anesthesiologist is impaired, and has a flirtation with nurse Olivia, only to be humiliated when he contracts syphilis. The outbreak spreads through the hospital, turning his private embarrassment into public gossip. Izzie remains the most emotionally porous of the interns, becoming attached to patients and repeatedly crossing lines because she cannot detach. Cristina remains the opposite, but even she is shaken by cases involving pregnancy and motherhood as she faces her own situation.

The Chief's health becomes a secret crisis when Derek discovers he has a tumor on his optic nerve after episodes of blurred vision in the OR. Derek, Bailey, and Meredith secretly operate on him at night, and Meredith is pulled deeper into the Chief's confidence. Around the same time, Meredith finally tells Derek the truth she has hidden from almost everyone: Ellis Grey is not away working but has advanced Alzheimer's. Derek comforts her, and their relationship seems to solidify just as the Chief warns Meredith to stay away from him.

By the end of the season stretch, the interns are more bonded but also more exposed. George learns that Olivia had previously slept with Alex and, realizing Alex is the source of his syphilis, punches him. Cristina is still pregnant and still hiding it. Meredith and Derek appear to be moving toward something real after all the secrecy, favoritism accusations, and emotional walls. Then everything detonates at once when a red-haired woman arrives at the hospital, confronts Derek, and introduces herself to Meredith as Addison Shepherd — Derek's wife.
